1. Vision

TokenRealty tokenizes real estate (buildings, flats, other property) so investors can buy fractional ownership and earn rental income using cryptocurrency (USDC, MATIC, ETH).

CapabilityDescription
Asset registryBuildings, flats, SPVs, valuations, legal documents
TokenizationERC-1400 property tokens on Polygon per flat
Primary marketInvestors buy tokens with crypto
Secondary marketP2P token trading with compliance gates
Rental operationsLease management, crypto rent, pro-rata dividends
ComplianceKYC/AML whitelist (off-chain + on-chain)
DocumentsTitle deeds, leases on IPFS
